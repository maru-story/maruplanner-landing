# Maru - Brand Identity (machine-readable brief)

Compact reference for AI agents and tools producing Maru assets. Full guide: `maru-brand-identity.pdf`. All asset files live in the owner's private Google Drive folder "Maru Brand Assets / Final assets" (folder: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ggj4DCHOvKfy7j32Qr-Dd90JAKvJKT3Q - private; requires access).

## What Maru is

Maru is a digital wedding planner (wedding website SaaS). Personality: an honest, trusted third person beside the couple - warm, reassuring, with some wedding elegance. It helps the couple prepare and is happy when they succeed.

## Logo system ("Flow")

M of soft twin arches with a gold heart at their meeting point. Original hand-drawn artwork, never re-set or redrawn. The "maru" lettering is artwork, NOT a font.

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Deep green | #2F4034 | Primary: logo arches/lettering, headlines, key buttons, mascot line work, groom's suit |
| Gold | #C9A35C | Accent only: logo heart, crowns, bouquets, highlights, badges, celebration moments |
| Ivory | #F5F0E6 | Home background; pages/cards may use #FDFCF8 |
| Soft sage | #A8BBA3 | Supporting: quiet surfaces, secondary panels, gentle separators |

Balance: mostly ivory, confident green, sage support, gold in small doses. Never introduce new brand colors. On dark green or photos, place the logo on an ivory card.

## Typography

- Display: **Fraunces** (semi-bold, generous sizes, never all caps) - headlines, empty states, celebration screens, print.
- Text: **DM Sans** (regular/medium) - UI text, captions, buttons, emails, documents.
- Never re-set the "maru" logo lettering in Fraunces or any font; always use the logo files as-is.

## Mascot

Rabbit whose ears follow the logo's twin arches with the gold heart at their meeting point; thin ear outlines balanced with the body contour. Sketch-style line character; shaped face (not a circle).

The pair has names: **Maru** is the groom rabbit, **Mari** is the bride rabbit. Calm and Lively are expressions of the same pair, not separate characters. Asset filenames keep the `maru-mascot-*` brand prefix for both characters; do not rename files after the characters.

Two registers, same characters and outfits:

- **Calm** (default): onboarding, planning prompts, empty states, shared couple screens. Files: maru-mascot-base.png, maru-mascot-bride-calm.png, maru-mascot-groom-calm.png, maru-mascot-couple-calm.png
- **Lively** (tilted heads, raised brows, motion marks; Maru waves): celebrations, greetings, social, stickers. Files: maru-mascot-bride-lively.png, maru-mascot-groom-lively.png, maru-mascot-couple-lively.png

Mari (bride): dress, flower crown, veil, gold bouquet. Maru (groom): green suit, white shirt, gold bow tie. Mixing registers is fine (Calm Mari beside Lively Maru still reads as the same pair). Mascot folder: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1-fi_rDPdpbVg1cu-fZ8yB2wDMyPHzo_P

## Usage rules

- Clear space: at least the height of the gold heart on all sides; nothing inside.
- Backgrounds: ivory is home; plain white OK; on dark/photo use an ivory card (no approved reversed version yet).
- Never: recolor arches/heart/lettering; re-set or redraw the lettering or shapes; stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows/effects; use files from "Explorations (not selected)"; use the mascot in place of the logo.
- Keep the ears' twin-arch + gold-heart motif and thin ear outlines balanced with the body contour; keep the sketch line (no vector-perfect smoothing without review).
- Extra poses/outfits are future proposals derived from the approved versions.

## Honest status (important for production decisions)

- All logo and mascot artwork is RASTER. Logo source is a single 1024x1024 image; lockups are crops/compositions of it; favicons are small by design.
- No SVG/vector versions exist yet. A faithful traced vector must be verified pixel-by-pixel against the original before replacing anything.
- Mascot PNGs are high-res on ivory backgrounds (not transparent). Transparent versions can be produced in a future vector pass.
- Current files are ready for screens, decks and social as they are.
